## Site Features

- Full bilingual AR/EN with one-click language toggle
- RTL/LTR auto-switching
- 10 services with 4-tab filtering (All, Build, Maintenance, Renovation)
- Quotation request form (service type, budget, location, details)
- Contact form (name, phone, message)
- 3 customer testimonials
- Scroll-triggered fade-up animations
- Mobile responsive (375px+)
- No phone/WhatsApp exposed — contact via forms only
